## Browser look parity (W1a and W1b)

- **OQ-WEB-GFX-PARITY** — `data/art_look.json` "compatibility" holds variant Environment overrides that apply on the web renderer. The values are marked `_placeholder` and are guesses for Shibi. W1b uses the Mobile property goldens and normalized shader source fixture to guard the reference look. Shibi's PC play test is the final check.
- **OQ-WEB-GFX-SKYLIGHT** — [measured] At exposure 1.7, a grey StandardMaterial3D lit by the procedural sky reads 0.420 red on Compatibility and 0.329 on Mobile. At 1.0 the readings are 0.235 and 0.239, respectively. Lowering Compatibility exposure to 1.35 brought the probe to 0.333. [inferred] Exposure affects sky-sourced light twice on Compatibility. The W1b data uses variant exposure and ambient fill to reduce the paving and stone difference in the match. These night (exposure 1.24, ambient 0.88) and storm (0.95, 0.65) values are a frame-wide compensation fitted to the parity sheets, not a measured sky-light fix: night and storm use colour ambient, and the lower exposure also dims the moon, emissives, sky and reflections (see OQ-WEB-GFX-GLOW). Neither value set was tried on the probe. Sky reflection parity remains open.
- **OQ-WEB-GFX-GLOW** — [measured] On unshaded HDR quads, 4x and 16x (0.1, 0.4, 0.6) read (0.737, 0.871, 0.914) and (0.925, 0.976, 0.992) on Compatibility against (0.729, 0.953, 1.0) and (0.953, 1.0, 1.0) on Mobile. W1b tried threshold, scale and intensity values and an HDR shader curve. The best threshold trial scored 9.31 at night and 13.78 in storm against controls of 9.34 and 13.75 at centre_standard. The shader trial scored 9.29 and 13.61. Bloom parity remains open.
- **OQ-WEB-GFX-DISC** — The sun and moon disc is a horizontal ellipse on Mobile and round on Compatibility at the same camera (porta_aquilae dusk). Cause unknown (the sky shader's disc uses EYEDIR only; the two renderers draw the sky background differently). Not changed in W1a.
- **OQ-WEB-GFX-GROUND** — W1b keeps W1a's ground conversion. [measured] With the W1b exposure and ambient settings, a Compatibility-only revert improved dusk porta_aquilae from 15.5 to 11.64 and dusk field_corner from 15.9 to 11.46, but moved dusk citadel_top from 2.30 to 6.86 and storm centre_standard from 13.75 to 17.39. Values are full-sheet mean absolute RGB differences on the 0–255 scale. Only these 4 of the 18 cells were measured with the revert.
- **OQ-WEB-GFX-BRAZIER** — [inferred] Compatibility's light falloff contributes to weaker, redder pools. [measured] An omni-only colour and energy trial moved night porta_aquilae from 11.52 to 11.58. The market control and trial both scored 15.96. Its nearest omni was 139.7 m away.
- **OQ-WEB-GFX-TRIM** — [unknown] The cause of the stronger close-up gold trim. [measured] Giving the bronze-layer surfaces linear albedo on Compatibility moved dusk soldier_closeup from 25.41 to 25.38, within capture noise.
- **OQ-WEB-GFX-RAIN** — [inferred] sRGB blending contributes to faint streaks. [measured] Doubling the streak alpha moved storm centre_standard from 13.75 to 13.77. Particle placement limits the whole-sheet signal.
- **OQ-WEB-GFX-PRESETS** — `data/graphics_presets.json` "pc_medium" (Medium) and "pc_high" (High) are guesses for Shibi; Low stays today's "default" preset unchanged. Each PC preset copies every art_* switch from "default" and turns no fx_* effect on. Which effects (if any) Medium should drop is open until the browser frame timings exist.
- **OQ-WEB-GFX-AUTO** — Auto on a PC or in a browser = High ("auto_pc": "pc_high"); a phone or any non-PC build keeps "default". A guess for Shibi: a weak laptop may need Medium; no hardware detection exists.
- **OQ-WEB-GFX-SHADOWS** — PC sun shadows: Medium = 2 splits (PSSM), 120 m, a 2048 px directional atlas; High = 4 splits, 200 m, 4096 px (today: orthogonal, 70 m, the project's 4096 atlas). Guesses; the split mode and distance take effect on the next match.
- **OQ-WEB-GFX-AA** — PC anti-aliasing and texture filtering: MSAA 2x (Medium) / 4x (High); anisotropic filtering 4x / 8x. Guesses; whether WebGL 2 honours each is checked by the parity captures.
- **OQ-WEB-GFX-FPS** — The Frame cap setting (default 60, rows 60 / 120, advanced 144 / Unlimited) and vsync on in both PC presets. The browser paces frames with requestAnimationFrame; whether Engine.max_fps caps anything there is measured later.
- **OQ-WEB-GFX-SCALE** — Render scale 50-100 percent in 5 percent steps (default 100), multiplying the preset's scale_3d (1.0 on PC). Hidden while OQ-WEB-GFX-ADVANCED is false.
- **OQ-WEB-GFX-ADVANCED** — `show_advanced_graphics` (false): whether Settings shows Field of view, Render scale and the 144 / Unlimited frame caps. A decision for Shibi.
- **OQ-WEB-FOV** — `data/camera.json` fov_setting: a saved Field of view replaces the aspect table's value (vertical degrees) within 60-95 in 5-degree steps; Auto ("") keeps the table. Guesses for Shibi.
- **OQ-WEB-FAR** — `data/camera.json` far_m 4000 m (Godot's default, now explicit) with far_margin_m 50 m: the 1 km map's far hills reach 3922 m from a ground corner (measured), so no shorter far plane is possible without cutting them; per-map far planes (alesia_500 needs about 2700 m) were not made. Depth precision on WebGL 2 depends on the near plane (0.05 m), not this value [inferred].
- **OQ-WEB-PERF-ROUTE** — `tools/web/perf_route.json` (W1a commit 4): the browser frame-rate harness's camera route on alesia_500 (drop 150 m over the south edge, the market, Porta Aquilae, the Arx top), its hold / travel seconds (8 + 20 + 14 + 12 + 14 + 12 + 10 = 90 s), arcs, warmup_frames 60, the cap probe (30 / 60 / 0 fps, 0.5 s settle + 5 s each), the per-variant seeds and the harness's 250 ms freeze line and 0.1 s route-clock step are TOOL DATA chosen by the harness author, not budgets and not game values; nothing in the game reads them. Whether this route is representative of play (it flies a free camera; bots and the local player do whatever the autosolo match gives) is open for Shibi. No budget or preset is tuned to its numbers.
- **OQ-W2-PORT** — The home-network relay (`tools/lan/gladius_lan.mjs`) listens on two ports from `data/net.json` "lan_relay": 47443 (HTTPS, for other PCs) and 47480 (plain HTTP, this PC only). Both are guesses for Shibi. They are high numbers that need no admin rights and avoid 7777 (the ENet port) and 8060-8099 (the web tools). Change them if another program already uses them.
- **OQ-W2-MSG-CAP** — The relay refuses a signaling message over 16384 bytes (16 KB) and keeps at most 8 sockets in the room (the host included; the value is `data/match.json` max_humans). Both are guesses for Shibi. A typical WebRTC offer is about 2 to 6 KB [inferred], so 16 KB leaves room; nobody has measured it.
- **OQ-W2-RELAY-LIMITS** — Relay values in `data/net.json` "lan_relay", all guesses for Shibi: `heartbeat_ms` 20000 (the relay pings each socket this often and drops one that missed the last ping, so a dead socket turns into `peer_left` or `host_gone` within about 20 to 40 s); `sockets_per_peer` 4 (at most max_peers × 4 = 32 open signaling sockets of any kind, joined or not; more are refused with 503); `cert_days` 825 (life of the self-signed certificate) and `cert_renew_days` 7 (a new certificate is made when fewer days are left). None is measured.
- **OQ-W2-BUFFER-FULL** — In the W2 gate's LAN smoke the guest logged about 90 `ERROR: Buffer full! Dropping data.` lines in two bursts, right after multi-second page freezes [measured]. Inferred cause: the browser's WebRTC receive buffer fills while the page is frozen, and Godot then drops data, even on the reliable channel, so lobby or match events can be lost without notice [inferred]. Levers: the W1c freeze fix (removes the cause), and the project setting `network/limits/webrtc/max_channel_in_buffer_kb` (raise it; Lanista's call, not the slice's). Whether it happens in normal play after W1c is [unknown].
- **OQ-W2-HTTPS** — The relay serves guest PCs over HTTPS with a self-signed certificate that it makes itself. Browsers only run the game on a secure page, and `http://<LAN IP>` is refused by the Godot web export (measured in the W2 spike, Chrome 157). So each guest PC sees a certificate warning once and must choose Advanced, then Continue. The host's own browser uses `http://127.0.0.1` and sees no warning. Alternatives for Shibi to decide: accept the warning, or set up a locally trusted certificate on each PC.
- **OQ-W2-TWO-PC** — LAN between two physical PCs is untested [unknown]; everything so far ran as two Chrome windows on one PC. In Chrome 157 on this PC the only ICE candidates are mDNS (".local") host candidates [measured], and the ICE config has no outside servers by design, so each PC must be able to resolve the other's ".local" name on the home network. Wi-Fi client isolation, blocked multicast or a firewall blocking UDP would stop the connection. There is deliberately no fallback: sending game traffic through the relay (WebSocket) would add its TCP stalls and is Shibi's call, not made here. Shibi's two-PC test decides whether anything more is needed.
- **OQ-W2-DEBUG-PING** — Debug tool values, not game tunables, used only by the LAN smoke in debug web builds: `scripts/net/net_debug_ping.gd` PING_INTERVAL_S 1.0 (guest ping cadence), REPORT_INTERVAL_S 5.0 (console marker cadence), MAX_SAMPLES 600 (kept ping samples); `scripts/platform/web_args.gd` AUTOJOIN_RETRY_S 1.0 and AUTOJOIN_MAX_TRIES 120 (the ?autojoin Join retries), AUTOHOST_START_DELAY_S 1.5 (the ?autohost lobby presses Start this long after the first guest is listed); `scripts/platform/platform_services.gd` RELAY_PROBE_TIMEOUT_S 5.0 (the /lan.json probe; this one is in the shipping path but only decides when to give up on finding a relay). Chosen by the slice author; nothing in play reads the first six. Shibi may leave them.
